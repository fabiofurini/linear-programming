## LP Problem $P51$

!!! chiave ""

    $$
    \begin{array}{rrrrrrrrrrrrrrrr}
    {\max} & {-} & {2\,x_1} & {-} & {\,x_2} &  &  \\[2ex]
    \text{subject to} &  & \, {x_1} & {-} & {x_2} & {-} & 3 \, {x_3} & {\le} & {-} 5 \\[2ex]
    & {-} & 3 \, {x_1} & {-} & 2 \, {x_2} & {+} & 2 \, {x_3} & {\le} & {-}2 \\[2ex]
    &  & {x_1,} &  & {x_2,} &  & {x_3} & { \ge} & { 0 }
    \end{array}
