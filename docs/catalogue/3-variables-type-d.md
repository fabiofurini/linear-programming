## LP Problem $P81$

!!! chiave ""

    $$
    \begin{array}{rrrrrrrrrrrrrrrr}
    {\max} &  & {\,x_1} & {+} & {\,x_2} & {+} & {\,x_3} &  &  \\[2ex]
    \text{subject to} &  & \, {x_1} & {+} & {x_2} &  &  & {\le} & {2} \\[2ex]
    & {-} & \, {x_1} & {+} & {x_2} &  &  & {\le} & {1} \\[2ex]
    &  & {x_1,} &  & {x_2,} &  & {x_3} & { \ge} & { 0 }
    \end{array}
