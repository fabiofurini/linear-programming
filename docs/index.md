Teaching material conceived and developed by **[Fabio Furini](https://fabiofurini.github.io/)**, associate
professor at [DIAG](https://www.diag.uniroma1.it/), Sapienza University of Rome.

**The course lecture notes, online**: linear programming problems, polyhedra, the simplex algorithm,
duality, advanced methods and modelling techniques, with definitions, theorems with their proofs,
examples worked out step by step and exercises with solutions.

## How each chapter is organized

Each chapter is one of the course notes, with the same numbers and the same colors as the
lecture notes: on the website and in the PDF you find everything in the same place.

<div class="grid" markdown>

!!! definizione "Definition"
    The precise meaning of a new concept.

!!! teorema "Theorem, proposition, observation"
    A result to remember, with its hypotheses.

!!! esempio "Example"
    A computation worked out in full, one step at a time.

!!! chiave ""
    **In green** the key point to take away.

</div>

??? dimostrazione "Proof — opens with a click"
    All proofs are included, but folded: first read the statement, then open
    the proof when you want to study it. The same holds for the solutions of the exercises.

## The parts of the course

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-vector-triangle: **1 · Linear functions, polyhedra and linear programming problems**

    ---

    Linear functions (gradient, Hessian and level sets), half-spaces and polyhedra, linear optimization problems with their optimal solutions, examples and properties, the canonical and standard forms with the graphical method, possible outcomes and equivalences between forms.

    1\. Linear functions · 2\. Half-spaces and polyhedra · 3\. Linear optimization problems · 4\. Linear Programming problems · 5\. Possible outcomes and equivalent formulations

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](lp-problems/index.md)

-   :material-stairs-up: **2 · The simplex algorithm**

    ---

    The simplex algorithm on dictionaries, optimality conditions, pivot rules and cycling, Phase 0 with the auxiliary problem. Examples worked out step by step and exercises with complete solutions at the end of each chapter.

    6\. Simplex algorithm · 7\. Optimality conditions and pivot rules · 8\. Phase 0 of the simplex algorithm

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](simplex/index.md)

-   :material-swap-horizontal: **3 · Duality and the dual simplex**

    ---

    The dual problem, weak and strong duality, the possible outcomes of a primal-dual pair, complementary slackness; the dual simplex algorithm, infeasibility certificates and re-optimization. Examples worked out step by step and exercises with complete solutions at the end of each chapter.

    9\. Duality theory · 10\. Dual Simplex algorithm

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](duality/index.md)

-   :material-cube-outline: **4 · Matrix methods, sensitivity and geometry**

    ---

    The revised primal and dual simplex, the primal-dual method, sensitivity analysis, the geometry of linear programming (faces, vertices and basic solutions). Examples worked out step by step and exercises with complete solutions at the end of each chapter.

    11\. Revised simplex algorithm · 12\. Revised dual simplex algorithm · 13\. The primal-dual method · 14\. Sensitivity analysis · 15\. The geometry of Linear Programming

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](advanced-methods/index.md)

-   :material-lightbulb-on-outline: **5 · Modelling**

    ---

    Techniques for writing a linear programming model from the description of a problem (production, blending, transportation, multi-period planning, linearizations), with worked examples. Complete models are in Exercises 5.

    16\. Linear Programming modeling techniques

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: The chapters](modelling/index.md)

</div>

- **[Mathematical Analysis 1](https://fabiofurini.github.io/mathematical-analysis-1/)** — real numbers, functions, limits, derivatives and series, with interactive graphs;
