# Downloads

The lecture notes and the exercise books in PDF, updated at every publication of the site, with the same numbering of definitions, theorems, examples and exercises as the website. Text and figures are released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en).

## Lecture notes

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 1 · Linear functions, polyhedra and linear programming problems**

    ---

    Linear functions (gradient, Hessian and level sets), half-spaces and polyhedra, linear optimization problems with their optimal solutions, examples and properties, the canonical and standard forms with the graphical method, possible outcomes and equivalences between forms.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-1-linear-programming-problems.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-1-linear-programming-problems.pdf)

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 2 · The simplex algorithm**

    ---

    The simplex algorithm on dictionaries, optimality conditions, pivot rules and cycling, Phase 0 with the auxiliary problem. Examples worked out step by step and exercises with complete solutions at the end of each chapter.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-2-simplex.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-2-simplex.pdf)

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 3 · Duality and the dual simplex**

    ---

    The dual problem, weak and strong duality, the possible outcomes of a primal-dual pair, complementary slackness; the dual simplex algorithm, infeasibility certificates and re-optimization. Examples worked out step by step and exercises with complete solutions at the end of each chapter.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-3-duality.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-3-duality.pdf)

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 4 · Matrix methods, sensitivity and geometry**

    ---

    The revised primal and dual simplex, the primal-dual method, sensitivity analysis, the geometry of linear programming (faces, vertices and basic solutions). Examples worked out step by step and exercises with complete solutions at the end of each chapter.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-4-advanced-methods.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-4-advanced-methods.pdf)

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 5 · Modelling**

    ---

    Techniques for writing a linear programming model from the description of a problem (production, blending, transportation, multi-period planning, linearizations), with worked examples. Complete models are in Exercises 5.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-5-modelling.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-5-modelling.pdf)

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Problem catalogue · Catalogue of LP problems**

    ---

    The linear programming problems used in the examples and exercises of the whole course, cited by their code P0--P86: problems with two and three variables, in four types (A: non-negative right-hand sides; B: dual simplex; C: Phase 0 needed; D: unbounded, infeasible or with multiple optima). For each, the problem and the figure of its feasible region.

    [:octicons-download-24: lp-problem-catalogue.pdf](pdf/lp-problem-catalogue.pdf)

</div>
